Descriptive and inferential analysis of clinical studies using Excel

In this academic research project, I conducted a comprehensive statistical and visual analysis of 7 clinical studies related to medical treatments using Microsoft Excel. The analysis involved:

📊 Descriptive statistics: Calculated mean, median, standard deviation, variance, kurtosis, skewness, range, and error metrics for patient count and follow-up duration.

🧮 Visualization: Created bar charts, pie charts, and summary tables to illustrate:

Distribution of publication years and article types

Frequency of injection methods and dosage reporting

Severity and duration of adverse effects

📈 Interpretation of statistical significance: Reviewed and interpreted p-values to assess treatment effectiveness across studies (5 out of 7 reported p < 0.05).

💡 Adverse event analysis: Summarized the types, frequency, and resolution time of side effects (e.g., edema, pain, facial asymmetry), showing that most were mild and transient.

This work enhanced my skills in data cleaning, statistical reasoning, and communicating results clearly using visual storytelling tools in Excel.
